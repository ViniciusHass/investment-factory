# INV-0001 — Define normalized market-data contract

**Status:** BACKLOG  
**Repository:** investment-factory  
**Spec:** `specs/002-financial-data-providers/spec.md`

Define provider-independent schemas for instruments, prices, financial metrics, FX, and data quality, with timestamps and provenance. No live API integration in this card.

The FX and price ports must remain independent of specific providers. See `docs/decisions/0004-market-data-ports.md`. This card remains in BACKLOG until the governing spec and owner decisions are accepted.
